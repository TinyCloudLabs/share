Apps for your TinyCloud

Every listing shows what the app can read and change, and pins the exact files it ships by checksum.

Opening an app saves its files to your TinyCloud if they aren't there yet. You see what it asks for first, and nothing is saved until you approve it in OpenKey. An app runs in a sandbox. Some browser requests can't be blocked. Only open apps you trust. Beta: use sample data only.
